HOW TO PLAY & CALIBRATION

Make sure your hand is visible and palm faces the camera. Hold your gesture steady!

✊
STONE (Rock)

Close all fingers into a firm fist. Beats Scissors, loses to Paper.

✋
PAPER

Open flat palm with all 5 fingers spread out. Beats Stone, loses to Scissors.

✌️
SCISSORS

Extend index and middle fingers in a "V". Beats Paper, loses to Stone.

Live camera feedback helps you position your hand for 100% recognition accuracy:

Lighting Estimation: Checking...
Hand Distance: Searching...

Best Practices: Keep your hand 1.5 to 3 feet from the lens. Avoid strong backlights or holding your hand at a steep angle.

Classic rules apply! Every round earns you XP and unlocks achievements:

• Quick Match: First to 2 wins.
• Best of 5: First to 3 wins or highest score after 5 rounds.
• Survival: Play until your first defeat. One loss ends the run!
• Time Attack: Win as many rounds as possible in 60 seconds!
